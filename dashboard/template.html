Free-Rider Index

Who pays for pharmaceutical innovation? Ten high-income countries, five measurable components, one adjustable score.

The index combines five published indicators. Higher scores mean a country pays less, waits longer, or invests less relative to the others. Move the sliders to change the weights; the ranking updates immediately.
